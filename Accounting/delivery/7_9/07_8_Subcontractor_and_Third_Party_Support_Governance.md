# Section 7.8 Subcontractor and Third-Party Support Governance

## 1. Principle
The prime bidder remains fully accountable for end-to-end service delivery, including all subcontractor and third-party contributions.

## 2. Governance Model
- Prime vendor as single point of accountability
- Clearly defined subcontractor scopes and obligations
- Unified SLA model across all parties
- Escalation chain controlled by prime vendor

## 3. Risk Controls
| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Third-party delay | Back-to-back SLA clauses and escalation terms |
| Knowledge silo | Documentation and handover obligations |
| Service discontinuity | Named backups and continuity plan |
| Security variance | Mandatory policy conformance and audit rights |

## 4. Contractual Controls Checklist
- SLA flow-down clauses included
- Confidentiality and data protection obligations aligned
- Incident participation obligations defined
- Exit and transition obligations defined
- Non-performance remedies defined

## 5. Operational Controls
- Joint incident bridge procedures
- Shared ticket visibility and accountability tags
- Periodic supplier performance reviews
- Corrective action plans for recurring failures

## 6. Service Continuity Requirements
- No interruption during subcontractor transitions
- Documented handover and knowledge transfer
- Verified backup resources for critical competencies
