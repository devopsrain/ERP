# Section 7.3 Helpdesk and User Support Model

## 1. Helpdesk Structure
A structured multi-channel helpdesk will support all AICC ERP users.

## 2. Support Channels
- Phone hotline
- Dedicated support email
- Online ticket portal
- Optional chat channel

## 3. Ticket Lifecycle
1. Ticket creation
2. Categorization and severity assignment
3. Triage and assignment
4. User communication and ETA
5. Resolution and validation
6. Closure and feedback capture

## 4. Severity Matrix
| Severity | Business Impact | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Business halt or severe risk | Complete outage, major security issue |
| High | Core function unavailable | Payroll run blocked, posting failure |
| Medium | Partial disruption | Report mismatch, non-critical function issue |
| Low | Minor inconvenience | UI issue, formatting issue |

## 5. Response and Resolution Targets
| Severity | Response Target | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | 15 minutes | 8 hours |
| High | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| Medium | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| Low | 1 business day | 10 business days |

## 6. Escalation Path
- L1 to L2: unresolved after first triage window
- L2 to L3: unresolved technical issue or repeat defect
- L3 to Management: critical issue breach risk

## 7. User Communication Standard
- Acknowledgment sent within response SLA
- Progress update every agreed interval
- Closure summary with root cause and preventive advice

## 8. Issue Tracking Fields
- Ticket ID
- Requester and department
- Module and feature
- Severity and priority
- Assigned owner
- Status and timestamps
- Root cause and corrective action
