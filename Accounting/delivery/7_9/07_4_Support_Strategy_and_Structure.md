# Section 7.4 Support Strategy and Structure

## 1. Strategy Objective
Define a sustainable support model that ensures continuity, rapid recovery, and long-term capability building at AICC.

## 2. Support Organization
| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Service Manager | Governance, SLA reporting, stakeholder communication |
| Support Lead | Daily support operations and queue oversight |
| L1 Analysts | User support and issue intake |
| L2 Engineers | Technical troubleshooting and remediation |
| L3 Engineers | Deep fixes, product-level corrections |
| Security Lead | Security incident coordination and hardening |
| DBA/Infra Lead | Data platform and infrastructure resilience |

## 3. Responsibility Split (RACI Summary)
| Activity | Bidder | AICC |
|---|---|---|
| Incident handling | R/A | C/I |
| Business validation | C | R/A |
| Change approvals | C | R/A |
| Major outage escalation | R | A |
| Knowledge transfer execution | R/A | C |

## 4. Escalation Procedures
- P1 incidents trigger immediate bridge call and management notification.
- Escalation levels follow L1 to L2 to L3 to leadership path.
- Incident commander assigned for every P1/P2 event.

## 5. Capacity Building Plan
- Joint shadowing during first support cycles.
- Reverse shadowing for AICC analysts.
- Quarterly skills assessment and refresher training.

## 6. Governance Cadence
| Cadence | Meeting | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Ops huddle | Incident review and assignments |
| Weekly | Service review | SLA trend and blockers |
| Monthly | Steering review | KPI, risk, and improvement plan |
| Quarterly | Strategy review | Roadmap alignment and capability maturity |
