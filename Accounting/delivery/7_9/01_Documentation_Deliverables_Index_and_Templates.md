## 6. Documentation Acceptance Criteria

- Every document has owner, version, and approval metadata.
- All module workflows are covered with screenshots or step instructions.
- All critical operations include rollback or recovery instructions.
- Security-sensitive procedures are clearly permission-scoped.
- Documents are stored in version control and access-controlled repository.
