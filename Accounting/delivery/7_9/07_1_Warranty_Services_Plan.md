# Section 7.1 Warranty Services Plan

## 1. Warranty Scope
This warranty plan applies to all delivered ERP components for a minimum of three years from final acceptance.

Covered components:
- Application modules and business workflows
- Configuration and customization deliverables
- Database schemas, procedures, and integrity controls
- Integration connectors and APIs
- Security controls and operational automations
- Third-party components supplied through the bidder

## 3. Warranty Service Inclusions
- Defect correction and bug fixing
- Security patching and hardening updates
- Performance tuning and optimization
- Minor enhancements and configuration adjustments
- Data correction and database issue resolution
- Root-cause analysis for recurring faults

## 4. Service Exclusions
- Scope expansion beyond baseline contract without change control
- Infrastructure replacement not included in delivered scope
- Non-approved third-party changes performed outside governance

## 5. Incident Prioritization
| Priority | Description | Response Target | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Production outage, data integrity risk, security incident | 15 minutes | 8 hours |
| P2 High | Major function unavailable | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| P3 Medium | Partial degradation, workaround available | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | Minor issue, cosmetic or low-impact defect | 1 business day | 10 business days |

## 6. Warranty Governance
- All incidents logged with reference IDs.
- Monthly warranty report submitted to AICC.
- Repeat incidents tracked and corrective actions assigned.
- Formal approval required for closure of critical incidents.

## 7. Responsibilities
### Bidder Responsibilities
- Provide qualified support engineers.
- Deliver fixes within SLA targets.
- Coordinate all subcontractor obligations.
- Maintain change records and rollback plans.

### AICC Responsibilities
- Provide authorized contacts and approval points.
- Provide timely access to logs, users, and environments.
- Validate resolution outcomes within agreed windows.
